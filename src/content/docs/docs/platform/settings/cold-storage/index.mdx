Cold Storage keeps a copy of your logs in an S3 bucket in your own AWS account, long after they've left KloudMate's log retention. When you need older logs again, you rehydrate a time range: KloudMate loads it back so you can search it in the Log Explorer for 7 or 14 days.

Use it when you need logs older than your plan's retention, for example to:

- keep a year or more of logs at S3 storage prices
- answer an audit or compliance request
- investigate an incident or security event that started before your retention window

Cold Storage archives logs only. Traces and metrics aren't archived. It's available on paid plans.

## How it works

1. **Archive.** An organization Owner connects an S3 bucket for the workspace. Every hour, KloudMate exports that hour's logs to the bucket as Parquet files. The files belong to you: they stay in your bucket, and you can query them with your own tools, such as Amazon Athena.
2. **Rehydrate.** When you need logs from the archive, pick a time range and, optionally, the services you're interested in. You see an estimate of the size, load time, and charge before anything starts.
3. **Search.** In the Log Explorer, switch the source from **Live logs** to the rehydration and search it like any other logs. When it expires, KloudMate removes the rehydrated logs. The files in your bucket aren't affected.

## Where to find it

Open **Settings → Cold Storage**.

- **Archive**: connect the bucket, check that exports are working, and see which days are in the archive. Only organization Owners can use this tab.
- **Rehydrations**: start a rehydration, follow its progress, and see when each one expires.

In the Log Explorer, the **Live logs** button switches between live logs and the rehydrations you can search. See [Search a rehydration](./rehydrate-logs/#search-a-rehydration-in-the-log-explorer).

## Who can do what

| Action | Owner | Developer | Viewer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Connect, verify, replace, disable, or delete the bucket | Yes | No | No |
| See the archive status and coverage | Yes | No | No |
| See rehydrations and search them in the Log Explorer | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Start, cancel, or delete a rehydration | Yes | Yes | No |

**Owner** is the organization role. **Developer** and **Viewer** are workspace roles.

## Pricing

- **Archiving** has no KloudMate charge. AWS bills your account for storing the files in S3.
- **Rehydration** costs $0.10 per GB, measured on the volume the workspace originally ingested for the hours you load, not on the compressed Parquet size. For example, loading a day in which the workspace ingested 50 GB of logs costs $5.00. The charge appears on your KloudMate bill as **Log Rehydration**.
- **Reading files out of S3** during a rehydration is billed by AWS as data transfer. The estimate shows the size that will be read from S3, but its charge covers only KloudMate's part, not AWS's.
