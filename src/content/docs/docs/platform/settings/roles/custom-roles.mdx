A custom role gives a person the permissions their job needs and nothing more. For example, an on-call engineer can acknowledge and resolve incidents without the right to change alert rules or escalation policies. You create the role once for the organization, then give it to members in any workspace.

## Before you start

- Your organization needs a paid plan. The built-in roles work on every plan.
- Only an organization **Owner** can create, edit, or delete custom roles.

## What a custom role can include

A custom role decides what a person can change, run, or share. It doesn't hide data:

- Every custom role has **Read** on every module except **Workspace settings**. You can't remove it.
- **Workspace settings** and **AI Assistant** are optional. Without **Workspace settings**, the person doesn't see the workspace settings pages. Without **AI Assistant**, they can't use the Assistant.
- A custom role holds workspace permissions only. Organization tasks, such as inviting users or managing billing, stay with Owners.

For what each permission lets a person do, see the [permission reference](../permission-reference/).

## Create a custom role

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings → Roles**.

2. Click **Create role**, then select **Blank role** or a template.

3. Enter a **Name**, and optionally a **Description**. Each role in the organization needs its own name.

4. Under **Permissions**, tick a module to give all of its permissions. To give single permissions, open the module and tick each one.

5. Click **Create role**.

</Steps>

Every **Workspace settings** permission comes with **Read**, and every **AI Assistant** permission comes with **Use**.

## Start from a template

A template is a custom role with permissions already picked for a common job. Select it from **Create role**, then change the name or the permissions before you save.

| Template | For | Permissions added to Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Responder** | On-call engineers who handle alerts and incidents | **Operate** on Alerts, Issues, Incidents, Synthetic monitoring, and Workflows. AI Assistant **Use** and **Investigate**. |
| **Incident manager** | People who run the incident process and talk to customers | **Operate** on Alerts, Issues, Synthetic monitoring, and Workflows. Incidents **Operate** and **Configure**. Status pages **Post** and **Manage**. AI Assistant **Use** and **Investigate**. |
| **Platform admin** | The team that runs data collection and integrations | Dashboards **Manage**. Logs **Rehydrate**. AI Assistant **Use**. Every **Workspace settings** permission. |
| **Auditor** | Security and compliance reviewers | Workspace settings **Read**. No AI Assistant. |

## Give a custom role to a member

You give a custom role the same way as a built-in role: when you [invite a user](../../users-permissions/#invite-a-user) or [change a user's roles](../../users-permissions/#change-a-users-roles). You can also make a custom role the [default for single sign-on](../../sso/provisioning-and-enforcement/#choose-the-roles-for-new-accounts).

## Edit or delete a custom role

On **Settings → Roles**, open the actions menu on the role's row:

- **Edit**: after you click **Save**, the change applies to every member with the role, in every workspace.
- **Delete**: the role is removed from every workspace. You can't delete a role that a member still has. Give them another role on [**Settings → Users**](../../users-permissions/) first.

## If your plan no longer includes custom roles

- Members with a custom role get **Viewer** access in that workspace.
- You can't create or edit custom roles, or give one to another member. A member keeps a custom role they already have, and you can still delete a role.
- When the plan includes custom roles again, each role applies again with its saved permissions.
