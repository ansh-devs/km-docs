How long KloudMate keeps your data depends on what it is. Logs and traces follow the retention your plan sets. Metrics are kept for a year. And several other windows are fixed in the backend and don't change with your plan, so a session replay recording still ages out in 7 days even on an account that keeps logs for a year.

This page lists every retention window, starting with the ones you can't see in the product.

## Plan retention

Logs and traces are kept for the retention window your plan sets. This is the data you can query directly in the Log Explorer and the trace views.

| Data | Free | Pro | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Logs and traces | 7 days | 14 days | Custom |
| Metrics | 1 year | 1 year | 1 year |

Metrics are billed by sample count rather than by how long they're kept, so their retention is the same on every plan and doesn't affect the price. When no plan retention applies to a signal, it falls back to 14 days.

To change your logs and traces retention, open [Subscription & Billing](../settings/subscription-billing/).

## Metrics are tiered by resolution

A metric isn't kept at full resolution for the whole year. Raw samples are kept for about 3 hours, then served from rollups at coarser resolution: a 15-day tier, a 63-day tier, and a long rollup kept for the full year. KloudMate picks the resolution for the range you query, so a chart over the last hour reads raw samples and a chart over the last quarter reads the long rollup.

## Fixed retention windows

These windows are fixed and don't follow your plan retention. Raising your logs and traces retention doesn't extend them.

| Data | Kept for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session replay recordings | 7 days | The rrweb recordings behind a RUM session. Not archivable to Cold Storage. |
| Continuous profiling | 7 days | |
| Anomaly forecast bands | 7 days | The predicted band a forecast alert compares against. |
| Issue occurrences | 30 days | |
| Metric cardinality analytics | 45 days | |
| Endpoint (RED) history | 90 days | An hourly rollup, kept so request-rate, error, and duration history outlives raw trace retention. |
| RUM route metrics | 90 days | An hourly rollup of per-route performance. |
| RUM custom events | 400 days | An hourly rollup, so you can compare this quarter against last quarter. |
| RUM release health | 400 days | An hourly rollup, so you can compare a release against an earlier one after their raw sessions have aged out. |

Session replay is the most surprising of these: an account on 1-year retention still keeps only 7 days of recordings.

Raw RUM data follows your plan retention, not these fixed windows. That covers sessions, errors, journeys, raw events, route visits, and the raw custom events behind the 400-day rollup.

## Keeping data longer

To keep logs beyond your plan retention, archive them to your own S3 bucket with [Cold Storage](../settings/cold-storage/). The archive lives in your AWS account at S3 storage prices, and you rehydrate a time range into the Log Explorer when you need to search it. Cold Storage archives logs only. Traces, metrics, and RUM aren't archived.

On the Enterprise plan, logs and traces retention is custom. [Contact KloudMate](https://www.kloudmate.com/contact) to set a longer window.
